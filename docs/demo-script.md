# Demo Script

## Demo Objective

Demonstrate how **RSU Campus Buddy (Smart Campus Chatbot with AI Image Recognition)** helps students, staff, and campus visitors identify university buildings, access student services, and navigate the campus efficiently using AI image recognition, an interactive campus map, and a searchable building directory.

---

| Khin Zu Zu Oo | Prototype Demonstration | Demonstrate the AI chatbot, image recognition, building directory, search, and campus map. |

---

## Demo Flow

| Step | Screen / Feature | What Presenter Says | Requirement ID | Expected User Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Landing Page | Introduce RSU Campus Buddy, explain the campus navigation problem, target users, and project objectives. | FR-01 | Users understand the purpose of the system. |
| 2 | Chatbot Homepage | Show how users begin by clicking **Try the Chatbot** and starting the AI-assisted workflow. | FR-02 | Users can easily access the main features. |
| 3 | AI Image Recognition | Upload a building photo and demonstrate how Google Teachable Machine identifies the building and displays related information. | FR-03, FR-10, FR-11 | Users can quickly identify campus buildings using AI. |
| 4 | Building Directory & Search | Demonstrate browsing the building directory and searching for buildings or services. | FR-05, FR-06 | Users can quickly find campus information. |
| 5 | Building Detail & Campus Map | Open a building detail page and demonstrate facilities, opening hours, floor plans, and campus map navigation. | FR-07 | Users receive complete building information and directions. |
| 6 | Admin Panel | Demonstrate how administrators manage and update building information. | FR-08, FR-09 | Campus information remains accurate and up to date. |
| 7 | Dashboard & Prototype Metrics | Display dashboard statistics such as chatbot visits, image recognitions, and mapped buildings. | FR-12 | Users and stakeholders can evaluate prototype usage and progress. |
| 8 | Closing | Summarize how RSU Campus Buddy improves campus navigation and invite the audience to test the prototype. | FR-16 | Audience understands the project's value and is encouraged to try the system. |

---

## Closing Statement

**RSU Campus Buddy (Smart Campus Chatbot with AI Image Recognition)** helps students, staff, and visitors navigate the university campus more efficiently through AI-powered building recognition, an interactive campus map, and a comprehensive building directory. By reducing confusion and improving access to campus information, the prototype provides a smarter and more convenient campus experience. We welcome everyone to try the prototype and share feedback to help us improve the system before the final release.

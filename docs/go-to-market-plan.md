# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name

**RSU Campus Buddy (Smart Campus Chatbot with AI Image Recognition)**

---

## Target Early Users

The first users of the prototype are:
- New and current Rangsit University (RSU) students
- International students
- University staff
- Campus visitors who need help locating buildings and student services

These users are most likely to benefit from AI-powered campus navigation and can provide valuable feedback for improving the prototype.

---

## Main Value Proposition

**Identify RSU buildings instantly with AI and access campus information, facilities, services, and directions in one place.**

---

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RSU Class LINE Group | RSU students | Navigate campus faster using AI | Click landing page and try the chatbot | Landing page views, chatbot visits | Most students communicate through class LINE groups |
| QR Code on Campus Posters | Students and visitors | Scan to identify campus buildings instantly | Scan QR code and open the landing page | QR scans, chatbot visits | Users can access the prototype while on campus |
| Instagram Story / Facebook Post | Student community | Discover AI-powered campus navigation | Visit landing page and upload a building photo | Story clicks, landing page views | Popular social media platforms among university students |
| University Orientation Events | New students | Learn campus navigation before classes begin | Try the chatbot during orientation | Demo attempts, feedback responses | New students need campus guidance the most |
| GitHub Repository & Project Demo | Lecturers and classmates | Showcase AI image recognition prototype | View project documentation and test the demo | Repository visits, demo views | Supports academic evaluation and project presentation |

---

## Launch Sequence

1. Complete the landing page and prepare the chatbot demo.
2. Generate a QR code linking to the landing page.
3. Share the prototype through RSU class groups, social media, and orientation activities.
4. Track landing page views, chatbot visits, and image recognition usage using local metrics.
5. Collect user feedback through a Google Form.
6. Improve the landing page, chatbot responses, and AI recognition model based on user feedback.

---

## Risk and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Users do not understand how the AI building recognition works. | Add a simple "How It Works" section with step-by-step illustrations. |
| Low user engagement with the landing page. | Improve the headline, CTA button, and visual design to encourage interaction. |
| AI misidentifies some campus buildings. | Retrain the Google Teachable Machine model with additional building images and improve the dataset. |
| Users upload unrelated or poor-quality images. | Provide image upload guidelines and sample building photos. |
| Limited feedback from early users. | Promote the prototype during classes and orientation events, and encourage students to complete the feedback form. |

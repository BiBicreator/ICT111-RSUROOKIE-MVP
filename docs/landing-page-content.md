# Landing Page Content

## Project Title
**RSU Campus Buddy: Smart Campus Chatbot with AI Building Recognition**

---

## Target User

The landing page is designed for:

- New Rangsit University students
- Current RSU students
- University staff
- Campus visitors

---

## Headline

### **Navigate RSU Campus Smarter with AI**

---

## Subheadline

RSU Campus Buddy helps students and visitors quickly identify campus buildings using AI image recognition or by searching the campus directory. Users can instantly access building information, facilities, and directions in one place.

---

## Problem Statement

New students and visitors often have difficulty identifying campus buildings and finding important facilities. They spend time asking for directions or searching manually, especially during their first weeks on campus.

Based on our user research (Lab 03) and prototype validation (Lab 08), users wanted a faster and more convenient way to identify buildings, search campus information, and navigate the university.

---

## Solution Description

RSU Campus Buddy provides an AI-powered campus assistant that allows users to:

- Upload a photo of a campus building for AI recognition.
- Search buildings through an interactive directory.
- View detailed building information.
- Access campus maps and directions.
- Quickly find university services and facilities.

## Responsible Data Message

RSU Campus Buddy only collects the information necessary to demonstrate the prototype during classroom testing.

Users should not submit sensitive personal information. Uploaded images are used only for AI building recognition during the demo and are not permanently stored. Any feedback collected will be used solely to improve the prototype for educational purposes.

# Final Reflection

## 1. What We Built

We developed **RSU Campus Buddy**, a web-based Smart Campus Chatbot with AI Image Recognition that helps students identify campus buildings by uploading a photo. The system provides building information, facilities, opening hours, student clubs, and directions. The prototype also includes a building directory, search function, dashboard, landing page, and administrator panel.

---

## 2. What We Learned About Users

Through interviews and user testing, we learned that new students and campus visitors often struggle to identify university buildings and locate important services. Users preferred a simple image upload process instead of searching through campus maps or websites. They also appreciated having all building information available in one place.

---

## 3. What We Learned About Requirements

We learned the importance of creating clear system requirements before development. Mapping every feature to the functional requirements (FR-01 to FR-12) made it easier to track project progress, organize development, and ensure that every implemented feature supported the project objectives.

---

## 4. What We Improved After Testing

After user testing, we made several improvements:

- Improved the landing page design and navigation.
- Added clearer descriptions for key features.
- Enhanced the dashboard with startup and acquisition metrics.
- Improved the building directory search experience.
- Added better validation messages and user feedback.
- Updated project documentation and requirement traceability.
- Planned additional AI training images to improve recognition accuracy.

---

## 5. What Was Difficult Technically

The most challenging part of the project was training the AI model using Google Teachable Machine. The recognition accuracy depended heavily on the quality and quantity of training images. Integrating the AI prediction results with the Flask backend and maintaining consistent building information also required significant testing and debugging.

---

## 6. What We Would Improve Next

For future development, we would:

- Train the AI model with more building images from different angles and lighting conditions.
- Increase recognition accuracy.
- Add indoor navigation between rooms and floors.
- Expand the building database to include every university building.
- Develop a responsive mobile version.
- Integrate Google Maps and real-time navigation.
- Connect the system with the official Rangsit University information system.

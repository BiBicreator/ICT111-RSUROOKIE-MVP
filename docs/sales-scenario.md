# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

**RSU Campus Buddy (Smart Campus Chatbot with AI Image Recognition)**

---

## 2. Target User / Customer

- **Primary user:** New and current Rangsit University (RSU) students
- **Secondary user:** International students, university staff, and campus visitors
- **Decision-maker or admin role, if any:** University administrator who manages and updates campus building information through the Admin Panel

---

## 3. Situation / Context

A new international student arrives at Rangsit University for the first week of classes. The student is unfamiliar with the campus and cannot find the correct academic building. Instead of asking other students or searching multiple websites, the student opens **RSU Campus Buddy**, uploads a photo of the nearby building, and receives instant information including the building name, available facilities, opening hours, student services, and directions.

---

## 4. Pain Point

Students and visitors often experience difficulty identifying campus buildings and locating important facilities. Campus information is scattered across different sources, causing confusion, wasted time, and missed appointments. University staff also spend time answering repetitive questions about building locations and services.

---

## 5. Product Introduction

During university orientation, a lecturer introduces **RSU Campus Buddy** as an AI-powered campus navigation assistant. Students are shown how to upload a building photo, use the interactive campus map, search the building directory, and access campus information through the chatbot.

---

## 6. Value Message

**For RSU students and campus visitors who experience difficulty finding buildings and student services, our RSU Campus Buddy (Smart Campus Chatbot with AI Image Recognition) helps them identify buildings and navigate the campus quickly by using AI image recognition, an interactive campus map, and a searchable building directory.**

---

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

*"Have you ever spent several minutes trying to find the correct building or asking people for directions on campus? RSU Campus Buddy makes campus navigation much easier. Simply upload a photo of any RSU building, and our AI instantly identifies it, then provides detailed information about facilities, services, opening hours, and directions. Whether you're a new student or a visitor, you can find what you need in just a few seconds."*

---

## 8. Expected User Action

- Try the Chatbot
- Upload a building photo
- Explore the interactive campus map
- Search the building directory
- Give feedback on the prototype
- Recommend the prototype to other RSU students

---

## 9. Related Requirements

- **FR-01** – Homepage / Landing Page
- **FR-03** – AI Image Upload and Building Recognition
- **FR-05** – Building Directory
- **FR-06** – Search and Filter
- **FR-07** – Building Detail View
- **FR-09** – Admin Management
- **FR-12** – Dashboard and Prototype Metrics
